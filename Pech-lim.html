My Story

A journey shaped by resilience and purpose.

My journey is rooted in Rhino Camp Refugee Settlement in Uganda, where I have spent more than two decades navigating the challenges of displacement, education and limited opportunities.

After performing well in primary school, I stayed home for two academic terms without joining secondary school because of financial challenges. In 2014, Windle International Uganda selected me for a scholarship that supported my education from Senior One to Senior Six.

During the scholarship orientation, I encountered a computer for the first time in a practical way. I had heard about computers before, but I had never touched a keyboard or understood how computers worked. That experience helped spark my interest in technology.

After completing secondary school in 2019, I spent four years without access to further education. In 2023, I received a Mastercard Foundation Scholarship to study Software Engineering at USIU-Africa in Kenya.

Today, I channel my experiences into supporting refugee and displaced youth to access education, mentorship, digital skills and opportunities.
